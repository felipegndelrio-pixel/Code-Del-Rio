NexoProcess — Refund Policy (DRAFT v0.1 — not reviewed by a lawyer)

DRAFT. Not for publication. Open points are marked TO VALIDATE.

Last updated: 01/11/2026 (draft)

1. Refund window

You can ask for a refund within 7 days of purchase.

TO VALIDATE (founder, before publishing): the founder set a 7-day refund policy on the product in Gumroad, and the Gumroad account default is 30 days. Which one buyers see at checkout was not confirmed. The text must match what Gumroad shows. If the account default applies, change "7 days" to "30 days" or align the product setting.

2. How to ask

Request the refund through Gumroad (the receipt e-mail has the link) or contact support using the purchase e-mail. TO VALIDATE: the exact path on Gumroad.

3. What happens to your access

  • After a full refund, access to NexoProcess ends on your devices at the next check.
  • A partial refund keeps your access.
  • A chargeback or an open payment dispute ends access. If the dispute is won by the seller, access returns.

4. Your work

Your processes are stored on your device. Export a backup before the refund if you want to keep them; the app does not delete your local data on its own.

5. Statutory rights

TO VALIDATE (lawyer): consumer-withdrawal rights outside Brazil (for example EU/UK cooling-off rules, US state rules). This policy does not restate them and does not limit them.
